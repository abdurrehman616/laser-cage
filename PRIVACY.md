# Laser Cage – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Laser Cage is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

## Information stored on your phone
Your coins, gems, unlocked items, level, settings and match stats are saved **only on your own phone**. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data. We don't run any servers and don't receive this information.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
Laser Cage shows **optional rewarded video ads**. You only see an ad when you choose to watch one (for example to double your coins or get an extra spin). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software collects and shares with Google:

- your device's **IP address** (which can show your approximate location)
- **app interactions** such as app launches, taps and ad views
- **diagnostic information** about app and ad performance
- **device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is sent encrypted. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads). In the UK, EU and some other regions, you're asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under **Settings → Ad privacy** in the game.

Learn more: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases (Google Play)
You can buy gems and other items with real money. Payments are handled entirely by **Google Play**. We never see your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what you bought so it can give you the item.

The **No-Ads Pass** lets you collect every ad reward without watching an ad.

## Children
Laser Cage is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes
If a future version changes what information is used (for example online play or accounts), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
