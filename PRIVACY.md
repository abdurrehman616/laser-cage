# Laser Cage – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Laser Cage is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The current version of Laser Cage does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

- Your coins, unlocked items, settings and match stats are saved **only on your own phone**. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.
- The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads and no analytics.
- The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Permissions
- **Vibration**: used for short buzzes during matches. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Internet**: included by the app framework. The current version does not send data anywhere.

## Children
Laser Cage is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Future updates
Later versions may add online play, ads or in-app purchases. If that changes what information is used, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
