How to play
  1. Remember. At the start of each round you see everyone for a moment.
  2. Hide & aim. Then everyone vanishes. Move somewhere new and aim where you think someone went.
  3. Reveal. At the lock, everyone appears and the lasers fire. Anyone touching a laser is out.
  4. Drop. The pink outer ring falls after every round. Don't be standing on it.

Tap the floor to walk. Drag the AIM pad to aim. On a computer, use WASD and the mouse.
