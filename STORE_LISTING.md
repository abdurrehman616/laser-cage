**Full description** (max 4000):
Everyone vanishes. Everyone aims. Then the lasers fire.

LASER CAGE is a fast party shooter where you can't see your rivals while you aim. Each round you get a quick look at where everyone is standing. Then they disappear. Move somewhere new, aim where you think someone went, and wait for the LOCK. When the lasers fire, anyone caught in a beam is out.

And the floor? It drops away ring by ring. Stand on the edge when it falls and you're gone. On the final platform there's nowhere left to hide.

HOW TO PLAY
• Remember: see everyone's position for a moment
• Hide & aim: everyone turns invisible. Move and aim with the twin sticks
• Reveal: lasers fire. Touch one and you're out
• Drop: the outer ring falls after every round

FEATURES
• Vs Bots: battle 1 to 5 computer players on Easy, Normal or Hard
• Smart bots that try to guess where you moved
• Shrinking hex arena with sudden-death final platform
• Shop: unlock laser colours, outfits, hats and floor skins with coins you earn
• Twin-stick controls made for phones, with left/right swap
• Plays offline, runs smoothly on most phones (Auto graphics mode)

Online battles with players from other countries are coming soon!
